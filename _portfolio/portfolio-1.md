The objective of this work is to develop an Autonomous Flapping Wing MAV capable of carrying a payload to a designated location. I developed a novel mathematical model that combines principles from both fixed-wing and flapping-wing dynamics. A key importance of my work is that this model extends existing fixed-wing designs, maintaining a similar overall architecture while allowing for updates to the lift parameters through my model.
