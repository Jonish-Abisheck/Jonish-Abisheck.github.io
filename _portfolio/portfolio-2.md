The objective of my project is to evaluate the path generated by our custom Global path planner and to validate it against established global path planners.

I was working on tuning the parameters on Local Path Planner (TEB) and was also tuning AMCL for smoother navigation. I developed two distinct tuning configurations: one focused on accurately following the global path, while the other allowed for necessary deviations. These configurations apply to any differential drive mobile robot.
